# 🧠 Built for Detection. Driven by Forensics. Logged for Impact.  

Hi, I’m **Nickalos** — but online, in the logs, and in the byte stream, you can call me **KvXbyte**.  

I’m a cybersecurity professional focused on **digital forensics and incident response (DFIR)**, with a strong background in SOC operations, log analysis, and threat detection. My path started with SOC-aligned labs and tools, but I’ve grown into **forensic investigation, adversary tradecraft, and incident reconstruction**.  

With a Security+ certification and the Google Cybersecurity Certificate already complete, I’m pursuing **CompTIA CySA+** and expanding into **DFIR certifications** (like GCFA/GCIH). My goal is to fuse tactical detection with forensic depth — not just stopping attacks, but **understanding how they happened, what they touched, and how to prevent them from happening again**.  

I specialize in **no-fluff, tactical execution**: dissecting packets, analyzing persistence mechanisms, or mapping adversary behaviors to frameworks like MITRE ATT&CK and the Unified Kill Chain. I build clarity from the noise and evidence from the traces.  

> *“No shortcuts. No fluff. Just precision.”*
